About Alabdaa

A trusted partner in business excellence and regulatory compliance across the UAE

Your Strategic Business Partner

Al Abdaa is a leading business service provider based in Dubai, dedicated to offering complete government and corporate support solutions under one roof. We specialize in simplifying business setup, visa processing, and government documentation, ensuring our clients experience a seamless and efficient process. At Al Abdaa, we work closely with key UAE government departments including FEWA, Tasheel, Ministry of Labour, Economic Department, and Civil Defense, ensuring accuracy, compliance, and timely service delivery. With a focus on professionalism, transparency, and customer satisfaction, Al Abdaa aims to be your trusted partner in all government and business service needs in the UAE

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the business setup process take in the UAE?

The duration depends on the business activity and required approvals. Most trade licenses can be issued within 3–7 working days once all documents are ready. Our team ensures timely submission and constant follow-up with authorities.

Can Al Abdaa assist with both mainland and free zone company setups?

Yes. We provide end-to-end assistance for both mainland and free zone companies — from choosing the right jurisdiction to completing license registration and visa processing.

Do you offer visa services for families and employees?

Absolutely. We handle family, domestic, and employment visas — including new applications, renewals, cancellations, and Emirates ID processing — ensuring complete compliance with UAE laws.

How does Al Abdaa coordinate with government departments?

We work closely with major UAE entities such as the Economic Department, Civil Defense, Ministry of Labour, Immigration, and FEWA. Our direct coordination ensures accuracy, speed, and smooth processing for every client.

Are your services transparent and secure?

Yes. We maintain complete transparency in pricing and process, with regular status updates. All client data and documents are handled securely under strict confidentiality protocols.

Why should I choose Al Abdaa for my government and business services?

Al Abdaa stands out for its professionalism, accuracy, and customer focus. Our certified team, deep government expertise, and commitment to timely results make us your trusted partner for all UAE business and documentation services.
